# JamBeacon Android test build — draft privacy notice and testing terms

NOT FOR PUBLICATION until the owner confirms the contact information, actual distribution configuration, network traffic, data inventory and applicable legal requirements. This draft describes only the local Android test build on this branch, not jambeacon.com or any other build.

## Privacy notice
JamBeacon is a music practice and avatar customization app. The test build uses your microphone only when you initiate a recording and grant permission. You may deny or revoke microphone access in Android settings; recording will then be unavailable. The test build mixes the microphone input with the selected backing loop. Saved recordings are stored locally using IndexedDB, with a limit of 20 saved takes; saving the 21st removes the oldest. You can delete individual saved takes in the vault or clear application storage to remove local recordings, avatar settings and progress. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may result in loss of recordings. Do not treat local storage as a backup.

The sample Beacon map and chat do not connect to other musicians or share your location in this test build. No payment is collected and no membership is activated. The test build has no production account creation, cloud music upload or public posting functionality verified. Any online services added later require a revised privacy notice, appropriate controls, an accurate Play Data safety disclosure and additional testing before release.
